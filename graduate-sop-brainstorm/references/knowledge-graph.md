# Applicant Knowledge Graph and Evidence Ledger

Build this before any prose. Update it after every answer. It is an internal model, not a section of the essay.

## Nodes

Record only what the student has actually given. Empty nodes are interview targets, not permission to guess.

1. **Background.** Who is this person, in the facts that matter to the application?
2. **Academic.** Training, fields, courses, methods, and what they actually learned how to do.
3. **Experiences.** The episodes that might carry a story. Include ordinary ones.
4. **Actions.** What the student personally did, not what the team or the lab did.
5. **Skills.** Capabilities formed through the work, stated only where an action shows them.
6. **Interests.** Problems they return to, not topics they say they "love."
7. **Turning points.** Events that changed a direction or a belief, including small ones.
8. **Tensions.** Contradictions, failures, mismatches, and things that would not resolve.
9. **Questions.** What they are actually trying to understand.
10. **Gaps.** Knowledge, method, or access they do not yet have.
11. **Goals.** Why graduate training is the next step, not a career slogan.
12. **Program fit.** Which resource in the target program answers which gap. Leave blank until both the gap and the program are known.

## Evidence ledger

Every important claim the essay might make gets a row.

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Claim | What the student wants the reader to believe |
| Evidence | The concrete experience that shows it |
| Reflection | How the student understands it |
| Relevance | Why it belongs to the target field |
| Confidence | Explicitly stated / Strongly supported / Needs clarification |

If Evidence is empty, the claim is **【Evidence Missing】**. You may mention the gap to the student. You may not write the claim as a fact in the draft.

Confidence meanings:

- **Explicitly stated.** The student said this in so many words.
- **Strongly supported.** Multiple concrete details point the same way, and you are not adding a motive they did not imply.
- **Needs clarification.** You can see a possible reading. Ask before using it.

## Current depth diagnosis

After each interview round, rate the leading experience on Scene, Action, Decision, Tension, Reflection, Question, and Academic Connection as strong, partial, or weak. The next round asks only the weakest high-value pair. Do not re-ask for background once it is strong.

Example: Scene strong, Action strong, Decision partial, Tension weak, Reflection weak, Academic Connection partial. Next round is Tension and Reflection only.
