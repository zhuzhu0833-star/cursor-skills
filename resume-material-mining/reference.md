# Resume Material Mining — Reference

## Universal questions (all target-relevant experiences)

1. What tools, software, lab equipment, or platforms did you use? What did you do with them?
2. What was your **independent** responsibility vs what you observed or assisted?
3. What was the **deliverable** (report, presentation, dataset, campaign, lesson plan, prototype, publication draft)?
4. **Quantify**: people served, items processed, budget, duration, % improvement, sample size, events, pages, users, revenue/cost (if applicable)?
5. Who did you collaborate with (teams, clients, patients, students, stakeholders)? Any conflict or bottleneck you helped resolve?
6. What **method or framework** did you apply (research design, statistical test, design process, consulting framework, clinical protocol)?
7. What **challenge or mistake** occurred and how did you respond?
8. Direct supervisor / mentor — name, title, recommendation willingness?
9. How does this experience connect to your **target program narrative**? (Ask after target field is known.)

## Education experience questions

- Dates, institution, degree, major, GPA (and trend if multi-year)?
- Core and elective courses **relevant to target program** — name, grade, key topics?
- Thesis / capstone / dissertation — title, methods, findings, status?
- Study abroad, exchange, summer school — courses and outcomes?
- Academic awards, dean's list, scholarships?
- If **cross-major applicant**: prerequisite or bridge courses taken; why the pivot; defining moment?

## Awards & honors questions

- Exact date, level (school/regional/national/international), rank or percentile?
- Selection criteria or competition scale (# participants)?
- Skills demonstrated — link to target field (e.g., analytical, creative, leadership)?
- Progression to higher-level honors?

## Activities (when section empty — always probe)

- Clubs, student government, volunteer — role, scope, duration?
- Competitions (case, moot court, hackathon, design, debate, math, etc.)?
- Events organized — attendance, budget, partners?
- Leadership: team size, responsibilities, measurable outcomes?

## Academic project questions

- Course name + project title?
- Individual vs group; your specific slice of work?
- Problem/question, method, tools, conclusion?
- Grade, presentation, or external use (client, publication, portfolio piece)?

## Research experience questions

- Project title, lab/group, advisor, dates?
- Research question and methodology?
- Your tasks: literature review, experiment, coding, fieldwork, writing?
- Output: manuscript, poster, thesis chapter, dataset, patent application?
- Co-author position if publication?

## Role-based internship / work question banks

Pick sections matching the student's **role**, then filter for **target-field relevance**.

### Corporate / business (general)
- Department and reporting line; products/services/markets involved?
- Business problem you addressed; analysis or operational work performed?
- Systems used (CRM, ERP, Excel, SQL, BI tools)?
- Client/internal stakeholder interaction?
- Recommendation or process improvement — adopted or not?

### Research assistant / lab
- Lab name, PI, project goal?
- Techniques, instruments, software (R, Python, SPSS, microscopy, etc.)?
- Sample size, trials, protocols followed?
- Contribution to paper, poster, grant, or dataset?

### Nonprofit / NGO / community
- Mission and beneficiary population?
- Programs supported; your role in delivery or fundraising?
- Events, outreach, or policy work — reach and outcomes?
- Grant writing, budgeting, or partnership development?

### Teaching / tutoring / education
- Setting (K-12, university, online); subject and level?
- Lesson planning, grading, classroom management, 1:1 support?
- Class size, sessions, student outcomes or feedback?
- Materials or curriculum created?

### Technology / engineering / data
- Stack, languages, frameworks; codebase or product context?
- Feature, bug, model, or pipeline you owned?
- Testing, deployment, documentation?
- Performance, accuracy, or user metrics?

### Design / media / creative
- Medium and tools (Adobe, Figma, Final Cut, etc.)?
- Client or brief; iteration process?
- Portfolio piece — audience, publication, metrics (views, engagement)?

### Healthcare / clinical (if applicable)
- Setting (hospital, clinic, public health); patient population (de-identified)?
- Scope of patient contact vs administrative/research support?
- Protocols, EHR systems, compliance training?
- Caseload, hours, quality or efficiency metrics?

### Government / policy / legal
- Agency/office; policy area?
- Memo, brief, legislative research, casework?
- Stakeholders; deliverables used in decisions?

### Short internship (< 2 weeks)
- Do not skip — ask for **one concrete task** and **one number**
- Intensity framing: what did you learn/deliver in limited time?

### Rotation (轮岗)
- Time per function; deepest contribution in which rotation?
- Cross-functional insight tying rotations together?

### Multi-site / international
- Relationship between sites; your mobility purpose?
- Cross-cultural or cross-regional comparison observations?
- Language use; global team coordination?

## Resume strategy questions

- Which experiences fit **1-page resume** for stated target program?
- Merge related entries (same employer, same theme)?
- Order: relevance to target program > recency > prestige?
- Top 3 competencies to emphasize (student-ranked)?
- Recommender candidates (faculty vs professional)?

## Program-type lens (adapt questions — do not assume one type)

After reading user's target programs, emphasize probes that match:

| Program type | Emphasize in questions |
|--------------|------------------------|
| Research / PhD-track | Methods, hypothesis, contribution to knowledge, publications |
| Professional / industry | Deliverables, business impact, tools, teamwork, leadership |
| Analytical / quant-heavy | Data sources, models, metrics, software, rigor |
| Creative / portfolio | Process, medium, audience, portfolio-ready outputs |
| Policy / public service | Problem definition, stakeholders, recommendations, scale |
| Clinical / practice | Setting, scope of practice, protocols, patient/community outcomes |
| Education / teaching | Pedagogy, assessment, learner outcomes, curriculum |

Map specific school names to these lenses using user's 选校表 — never hardcode school-program pairs in the skill.

## Field jargon

When student or intake form uses domain terms the advisor may not know:

1. Ask student to define in plain language.
2. Optionally web-search or explain term briefly when drafting questionnaire.
3. Do **not** maintain a fixed glossary for one industry in this skill — terms vary by major.

Common **resume-writing** terms (major-agnostic):

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Deliverable** | Tangible output you produced |
| **Scope** | Scale/boundary of your responsibility |
| **Stakeholder** | Person/group affected by or involved in the work |
| **Metric / KPI** | Measurable indicator of performance or impact |
| **Transferable skills** | Skills applicable across fields (communication, analysis, leadership) |
